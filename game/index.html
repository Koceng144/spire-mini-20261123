SPIRE MINI

Climb 3 floors. Build your deck. Beat the Warden.

How to play

  • Energy: each turn you get 3 energy and draw 5 cards.
  • Play cards: click a card, drag it onto the enemy, or press 1–9.
  • Read the intent: the badge above the enemy shows its next move — block big attacks!
  • Win battles to draft 1 of 3 new cards, or upgrade one you own.
  • End turn with the button or Enter. Beat the boss on floor 3 to win the run.
